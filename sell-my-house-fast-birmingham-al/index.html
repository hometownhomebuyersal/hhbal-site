Cash offers, as-is, in Birmingham

Birmingham is Alabama's largest city, and selling a house here the usual way can mean weeks of showings, repairs, and waiting on a buyer's loan. We are a family-owned, Springville-based company that buys Birmingham homes for cash as a principal, not as your listing agent. The cash price we agree on is the cash you walk away with: no agent commissions and, in most cases, we cover the normal closing costs.

We buy houses throughout the Birmingham area, from older neighborhoods inside the city to nearby communities along I-65, I-20, and I-59. Condition is not a barrier. Leave the house exactly the way it is. No clean-out, no showings, and no repairs before closing. Once we have looked at the property, we often make a fair cash offer the same day. Closings usually happen in as little as 7-14 days, depending on title work, and you choose the move-out date.

Situations we help with in Birmingham

We buy houses as-is regardless of condition. If one of these is your situation, you can still request a fair, no-obligation cash offer.

Inherited or probate homes

If you inherited a Birmingham house or you are settling an estate in Jefferson County, you do not have to clean it out or get it ready for the market. We buy inherited and probate properties as-is and can work toward a closing timeline that fits the estate.

Foreclosure or behind on payments

If mortgage payments have fallen behind or a foreclosure notice has arrived, a cash sale can close on a schedule you choose, often in as little as 7-14 days depending on title. There is no obligation if the offer is not a fit.

Divorce or a shared property

When two owners need to sell and move on, a straightforward cash offer can avoid a long listing. We buy the home as-is and close with a local title company when you are ready.

Relocation

A job change or a move out of Birmingham should not leave you stuck with a vacant house or a second set of bills. You pick the closing date so the sale can line up with your move.

Tired landlords

If rental income no longer covers repairs, tenants, or management in Birmingham, you can sell as-is. You do not have to evict, renovate, or list the property first.

Fire, foundation, or major repairs

Storm damage, fire, foundation issues, or years of deferred maintenance do not have to be fixed before you sell. We buy damaged Birmingham houses as-is and still make you a fair offer.

Behind on taxes or facing a tough decision

If the house has become more than you want to carry, fill out the form or call. We'll look at the property and give you a no-obligation cash offer so you can decide with clear numbers.

Frequently asked questions

How fast can you close on a house in Birmingham?

Usually in as little as 7-14 days, depending on title work. If you need more time, you pick the date that works for you.

Do I have to make repairs or clean out a Birmingham house first?

No. We buy as-is. Take what you want and leave the rest. We'll handle it.

Are there any fees or commissions?

No. There are no agent commissions and no hidden fees, and in most cases we cover the normal closing costs.

Will you buy a Birmingham house with foundation problems or fire damage?

Yes. We buy houses as-is regardless of condition, including homes that need major repairs. Leave it the way it is and we'll still make you a fair offer.

How do you decide on my offer?

We look at your home's location, condition, needed repairs, and recent nearby sales. Then we give you a fair, no-obligation cash offer, often the same day.

Do I need a realtor to sell my Birmingham house for cash?

No. We buy property as a principal, so there is no realtor required and no listing. You may still wish to consult a licensed agent or attorney before you sell.

Is there any obligation if I request an offer?

None. If our offer doesn't work for you, just say no thanks.

What parts of Birmingham do you buy in?

We buy houses in Birmingham and the surrounding area. If you are not sure your neighborhood is included, call or text 205-914-4215 and ask.
